# Digest style signals from this session

Use this as a compact bank of concrete editorial corrections for personal digests.

## What the user explicitly wanted

- Greeting line should be present when the format expects it: «Доброе утро, Миша!»
- Weather should appear clearly: temperature and rain/no rain.
- Recommendations should be dynamic for the actual day.
- Valid recommendation categories include not only tasks, but also music, film/series/video, walk, route, event, and practical daily actions.
- The user cared more about the text sounding alive than about squeezing in every possible “useful” detail.

## What repeatedly failed

- Generated symmetry and over-ordered structure.
- Decorative or explanatory filler.
- Abstract formulations that sound safe but not human.
- New synthetic phrases appearing after old ones were banned.
- Raw URLs pasted into the message.
- Replacing one awkward template with another instead of changing the editorial process.
- Keeping the latest version instead of the best version.
- Sending a digest too close to yesterday's result.
- Over-correcting into a dry task-only note and losing the useful human feel of a good content link.

## Phrases and patterns that triggered pushback

- «без распыления»
- «без перегруза»
- «без лишнего»
- constructions of the form «..., а не ...»
- «поддерживающая вещь»
- «бытовой сброс»
- «конкурентный рабочий результат»
- «рабочий блок»
- «собрать базу»
- «ветки»
- «хвосты» in digest prose
- «держать в голове»
- «вязкая задача»
- «что поедет с тобой»

## Strong user guidance

- Do not overfocus on the literal task list when the user is reviewing the format.
- Evaluate and improve from the angle of “generated / not alive”.
- Prefer short direct lines.
- After a rewrite, the text should go back to review again. This is a loop, not a one-time polish pass.
- Build several candidates, not just one.
- Keep the best-scoring candidate, not the most recent one.
- Compare the candidate against yesterday's final digest before delivery.
- Good links to video, music, route, or events are welcome when they make the message feel more alive.

## Editorial heuristic

When in doubt, cut:
- the explanation
- the qualifier tail
- the balancing clause
- the raw link
- the “smart” adjective

Keep the sentence that still sounds natural in chat.

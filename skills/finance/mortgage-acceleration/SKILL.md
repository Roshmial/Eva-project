# Mortgage Acceleration

## Purpose

Help the user evaluate practical ways to accelerate mortgage closure without undermining financial stability or quality of life.

This skill supports strategy comparison, scenario analysis, and prioritization.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:
- deciding how to accelerate mortgage payoff;
- comparing extra payments vs liquidity retention;
- balancing savings, income growth, and debt repayment;
- evaluating trade-offs around refinancing, reserves, and overpayment;
- turning a broad goal into a practical strategy.

## Inputs

When available, gather:
- current mortgage payment;
- rate and remaining term;
- current reserve/cash buffer;
- current monthly surplus;
- irregular extra income sources;
- risk tolerance;
- target payoff horizon.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the user's objective:
   - reduce total interest,
   - close earlier,
   - reduce stress,
   - improve cash-flow flexibility,
   - combine several goals.
2. Identify constraints:
   - required reserve,
   - income stability,
   - family/life obligations,
   - major expected expenses,
   - stress tolerance.
3. Compare realistic strategies, such as:
   - regular extra payments,
   - periodic lump-sum payments,
   - stronger expense optimization,
   - income increase,
   - hybrid strategy,
   - refinancing or restructuring if relevant.
4. For each strategy, analyze:
   - expected financial effect,
   - liquidity impact,
   - implementation difficulty,
   - sustainability,
   - risks.
5. Produce the answer in this structure:
   - short conclusion,
   - strategic options,
   - strongest approach,
   - key risks,
   - suggested first move.

## Rules

- Do not recommend aggressive overpayment if it destroys the emergency buffer.
- Do not assume that maximum speed is always the best choice.
- Treat psychological load and stability as valid factors.
- Make the link between spending changes, income changes, and mortgage horizon explicit.

## Pitfalls

- Do not focus only on mathematical payoff speed.
- Do not ignore income volatility.
- Do not recommend a plan that is too fragile to sustain.
- Do not confuse nominal savings with actually durable strategy.

## Verification

Check that:
- the mortgage goal is clear;
- constraints are explicit;
- strategies are compared, not just listed;
- liquidity and stability are considered;
- the next move is actionable.

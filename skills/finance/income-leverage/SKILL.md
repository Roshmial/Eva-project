# Income Leverage

## Purpose

Help the user identify the most realistic and effective paths to increase income, with attention to effort, strategic fit, risk, and sustainability.

The focus is not on fantasy income ideas, but on leverage points that fit the user's capabilities, experience, and constraints.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:
- increasing current income;
- comparing salary growth vs consulting vs side projects;
- evaluating income opportunities;
- identifying high-leverage career and monetization moves;
- prioritizing where to invest time for financial gain.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the user's current baseline:
   - main income source,
   - time constraints,
   - energy constraints,
   - skill base,
   - current optional opportunities.
2. Identify candidate income levers, such as:
   - role change,
   - promotion,
   - compensation negotiation,
   - consulting/advisory work,
   - packaged expertise,
   - training/mentoring,
   - digital product or service,
   - other realistic monetization paths.
3. Evaluate each lever by:
   - upside potential,
   - time to first result,
   - execution difficulty,
   - credibility/fit,
   - sustainability,
   - reputational risk,
   - conflict with main job,
   - energy cost.
4. Rank options by realistic leverage, not fantasy upside.
5. Produce the answer in this structure:
   - short conclusion,
   - best leverage options,
   - weak or misleading options,
   - recommended priority order,
   - next step.

## Rules

- Prefer realistic leverage over speculative side hustles.
- Do not assume more income automatically means a better path.
- Account for time, focus, and recovery cost.
- Tie income strategy back to broader goals, including mortgage closure and career trajectory.

## Pitfalls

- Do not overvalue ideas with low execution probability.
- Do not ignore main-job performance and burnout risk.
- Do not suggest random monetization ideas that do not fit the user's profile.
- Do not confuse visibility with monetizability.

## Verification

Check that:
- the user’s constraints are acknowledged;
- the options are compared on real criteria;
- the ranking reflects feasibility, not hype;
- the answer leads to a practical next action.

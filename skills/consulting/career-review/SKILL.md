# Career Review

## When to Use
Use this skill for:
- comparing offers;
- deciding whether to stay or leave;
- choosing between expert and leadership tracks;
- compensation and role evaluation;
- professional positioning and long-term strategy.

## Procedure
1. Clarify the user's real objective:
   - income,
   - influence,
   - stability,
   - learning,
   - autonomy,
   - status,
   - long-term positioning.
2. Clarify the time horizon:
   - immediate,
   - 6-12 months,
   - 1-3 years.
3. Analyze the situation as a business case using these dimensions:

### 1. Material side
- base salary;
- total compensation;
- bonus structure;
- long-term incentives;
- benefits.

### 2. Professional growth
- technology stack or domain relevance;
- scale and complexity;
- quality of learning curve;
- exposure to stronger problems or environments.

### 3. Career trajectory
- likely path in 2-3 years;
- management vs expert development;
- market signaling value of the company and role.

### 4. Work-life balance and culture
- remote/office/hybrid;
- schedule flexibility;
- overtime risk;
- autonomy vs micromanagement;
- process maturity vs chaos.

### 5. Risks
- company stability;
- political risk;
- burnout risk;
- role ambiguity;
- weak manager or unclear mandate.

4. Compare realistic options across these dimensions.
5. Produce the answer in this structure:
   - short conclusion,
   - comparison breakdown,
   - strongest upside,
   - hidden risks,
   - recommendation,
   - next step.
6. If the user has not organized the information, offer:
   - free dump mode,
   - guided interview mode.

## Pitfalls
- Do not reduce the choice to salary only.
- Do not assume a more senior title is always better.
- Do not ignore energy cost and reputation effects.
- Do not ignore time horizon.

## Verification
Check that:
- the objective hierarchy is clear;
- the options are compared on multiple dimensions;
- hidden risks are explicit;
- the recommendation is actionable.

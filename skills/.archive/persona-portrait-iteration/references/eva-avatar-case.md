# Eva avatar case

## Situation
The user iterated an assistant/persona portrait for recurring use in career and resume conversations.

## Durable learnings
- Once the user says the woman herself is ideal, preserve the subject almost 1:1 and move changes to the background, smile, realism level, and minor wardrobe adjustments.
- "Formal" does not mean luxury boardroom glamour. It can mean cleaner, more businesslike, and more structured while still warm and alive.
- The user may prefer a dark blazer with a clearly light top underneath because it preserves contrast and seriousness without becoming stiff.
- The user may reject a result as "too simple" even when it is warm; in that case the target is usually warmth plus status plus slight femme-fatale energy.
- If the user provides a reference smile, reproduce that expression explicitly instead of merely saying "friendly" or "warm".
- When a portrait feels too artificial, add realism cues directly: natural human photography, believable skin texture, candid-professional feeling, premium but real.
- If the user declares a version final, stop iterating.

## Practical phrasing that worked
- keep the woman almost 1:1
- soft warm closed-mouth smile
- formal but more alive and believable
- not luxury-staged, not expensive-glossy
- clean laptop without stickers
- no cars, no toys, no clutter
- slightly magnetic, but still approachable and warm

## Name-energy mapping from this case
- Liza drifted toward "too simple" for this user.
- Eva matched better once the image combined warmth, beauty, status, and a light dangerous/rocky edge without becoming cold.

## Use case fit
Best used for:
- assistant avatars;
- founder/consultant portraits;
- polished personal-brand portraits where the person should feel real, warm, and credible rather than glossy or generic.
